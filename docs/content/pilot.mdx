The CodeRocket UI editor, component catalogue and editable source export are free. **Pro adds more AI generations**, using the same tools and components.

[Request Pro access →](/contact?intent=pro)

## Choose your AI allowance

|                                      | Free                                     | Pro                                                    |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Editor, components and source export | Included                                 | Included                                               |
| AI generations                       | 5 trial generations in total per account | 100 per UTC calendar month while paid access is active |
| Activation                           | Create an account                        | Arranged personally after payment                      |
| Payment                              | None                                     | Price and access period agreed with the owner          |

The Free trial does not reset daily or monthly. You can continue editing manually and exporting source after using it.

Pro's AI allowance resets at the beginning of each calendar month in UTC. Unused generations do not carry over. Pro is active only for the agreed paid period; a monthly quota reset does not extend that period.

**Both plans are subject to a shared service limit.** AI can be temporarily unavailable when that limit is reached or when the provider is unavailable. The manual editor and export remain usable.

## How a Pro subscription works

1. [Request Pro access](/contact?intent=pro). Use the email address of your CodeRocket UI account, if you already have one, and tell us how you plan to use it.
2. We reply personally to agree on the price, payment arrangements and access period. There is no fixed public price at this stage.
3. After payment, we activate Pro manually on your account for that period. Your account badge shows your current plan and remaining AI allowance.
4. To continue after the agreed end date, arrange a renewal with us. There is no automatic billing or automatic renewal.

Sending the form is only an enquiry. It does not charge you, activate Pro or start a subscription. Your saved libraries and source export remain available when paid access ends.

## Need integration help instead?

We can also discuss a focused piece of work around your design system or React or Vue integration. Select **Help with an integration** in the [contact form](/contact?intent=integration). This is a separate conversation about scope and price; hands-on services are not included automatically in Pro.

## Framework availability

**React and Vue are available today.** Svelte and SolidJS support is planned, with no announced release dates. [Register your framework interest](/contact?intent=framework) to explain what you need. Buying Pro does not provide an unreleased framework implementation or guarantee a release date.

## What happens to your request

Your name, email and project details are saved in our private administration workspace. The owner receives a notification and reviews the request personally, then replies using the email address you provided. No response time is guaranteed.

We ask for your permission to contact you about that enquiry. The form does not sign you up for a newsletter or an automatic sales sequence. Please leave out passwords, API keys and confidential client data.
